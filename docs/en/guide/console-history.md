# Console and History

## Console

The **Console** in the bottom panel records every request actually sent, whether from the editor, a workflow run or the AI through MCP. Open an entry to see:

- the URL, headers and body actually sent (with variables replaced);
- the response status, headers and body.

JSON and XML request and response bodies have a format button to switch between formatted and as sent or received, and the copy button copies what is shown; wide content can be expanded horizontally. Click **Clear** to clear the Console.

The Console keeps the requests of the last 2 weeks, up to 100; each entry keeps up to 256 KB of request body and of response body. Bodies are stored in their own files and read when you open an entry.

**Find** in the bottom panel finds saved requests by name or URL; click a result to open it.

## History

**History** in the sidebar lists the requests you sent, grouped by day (Today, Yesterday and earlier dates), with the method, URL and status:

- Double-click an entry to open that request in a new tab as an unsaved request, which you can change and send again or save.
- The search box at the top filters the history by URL or method.
- Click the clear button to clear all history.

History also keeps the last 2 weeks, up to 100 entries.
